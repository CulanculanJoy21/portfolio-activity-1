Research / Capstone Development

Motorcycle-Mounted Road Anomaly Detection & Geospatial Mapping System

Role: Student Researcher / System Developer

In Development

Problem Statement

Road anomalies such as potholes and uneven surfaces can affect motorcycle safety and travel quality. The project explores a practical way to detect road-surface anomalies from motorcycle motion and associate detections with geographic locations.

Technologies / Tools Used

LIS3DH 3-Axis AccelerometerSensor DataGeospatial MappingMotorcycle-Mounted Sensing

Key Contributions

  • Developed the research concept around sprung-mass acceleration and road anomaly detection.
  • Defined the use of a LIS3DH accelerometer as the primary motion-sensing component.
  • Planned the connection between anomaly detection and geospatial mapping.
  • Worked on framing the system for evaluation as an academic capstone.

Outcome & Impact

The project is an ongoing capstone initiative intended to produce and evaluate a motorcycle-based road anomaly detection and mapping prototype. Its potential value is supporting road-condition awareness and future community mapping applications.
