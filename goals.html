Career Goals Portfolio

Building toward a career where technology solves real problems.

My goal is to grow from a student developer into a reliable IT professional with strong software, cloud, networking, and communication skills.

1–2 Years

Short-Term

Build a strong professional foundation

  • Complete my BS Information Technology degree and strengthen my academic portfolio.
  • Build and deploy several polished web and mobile projects that demonstrate practical skills.
  • Improve JavaScript, front-end frameworks, backend/API development, SQL, and Git workflows.
  • Gain deeper hands-on AWS experience and prepare for an entry-level cloud certification.
  • Seek an internship, freelance work, or junior IT/developer role to gain production experience.
3–5 Years

Mid-Term

Become an independent software professional

  • Work as a full-stack, mobile, cloud, or software developer on real products and services.
  • Design, build, test, and deploy production-ready applications with less supervision.
  • Earn relevant certifications in cloud computing, networking, or software development.
  • Develop stronger system-design, cybersecurity, DevOps, database, and collaboration skills.
  • Contribute to team decisions, support junior teammates, and take ownership of larger features.
7–10 Years

Long-Term

Lead impactful technology projects

  • Grow into a senior software engineer, solutions architect, cloud engineer, or technical lead role.
  • Lead the design of reliable systems and help teams make practical technical decisions.
  • Mentor younger developers and share knowledge through documentation, reviews, and training.
  • Build or contribute to products that improve how people work, learn, communicate, or access services.
  • Maintain continuous learning so my skills remain relevant as technology evolves.
